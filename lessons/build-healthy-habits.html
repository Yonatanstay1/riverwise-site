LESSON 06 · MINDSET & LIMITS · 4 MIN

Build healthy habits

Separate decisions from outcomes and keep learning in perspective.

Decisions are not outcomes

Poker has variance: strong hands can lose and weak decisions can win. Review what you knew at the time, the range you considered and your reasoning. Avoid judging a choice only by the next card or the final result.

Recognize tilt

Tilt is a state in which frustration, urgency or excitement takes over decision-making. Signs include rushing, chasing a loss and ignoring limits. Step away when you notice those patterns. A break is a useful decision, not a failure.

Set boundaries before starting

Keep a time limit for study and practice. Sopavo uses no real money. If you ever choose to gamble elsewhere, follow local laws and age restrictions, use only an entertainment budget you can afford to lose and never borrow or chase losses. Gambling is not an income plan. Independent support is available through Gambling Therapy.
