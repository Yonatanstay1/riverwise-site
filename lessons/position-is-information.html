LESSON 03 · TABLE AWARENESS · 5 MIN

Position is information

See why where you sit changes how much you know.

Acting later helps

Position describes when you act relative to other players. Acting after an opponent lets you see their action before choosing your own. That extra information can help you control the pot, decide whether to bet and respond to changing board cards.

Before and after the flop

Pre-flop, the first active player to the left of the big blind usually acts first. After the flop, action starts with the first active player to the left of the dealer button. In heads-up play, the button is the small blind, acts first pre-flop and acts last on later streets.

A starting point, not a rigid rule

From early position, more players remain to act, so begin with stronger starting hands. Later position can support a wider opening range, depending on stack depth, prior action and opponents. Position does not make every hand playable or every bluff good.

TAKE THIS WITH YOUBefore making a decision, ask: who still has to act, and will I have position on later streets?
