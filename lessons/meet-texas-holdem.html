LESSON 01 · THE BASICS · 4 MIN

Meet Texas Hold’em

Two private cards. Five shared cards. One clear place to start.

The goal of a hand

In Texas Hold’em, each player receives two private cards. Five community cards are dealt face up in stages. At showdown, each player makes the best five-card poker hand using any combination of their two cards and the five community cards. You may use both, one or neither of your private cards.

How a hand unfolds

The small and big blinds create an initial pot. Players act before the flop, then after the three-card flop, the one-card turn and the one-card river. On each betting round, a player can check when no bet is outstanding, or bet. Facing a bet, the usual choices are call, raise or fold.

Winning a pot

If all other players fold, the remaining player wins without showing a hand. If two or more remain after the final betting round, their hands are compared. Equal five-card hands split the pot. The dealer button moves one seat clockwise for the next hand.

TAKE THIS WITH YOUYour hand is always the best five cards available to you. You do not have to use both private cards.
A QUICK CHECK

How many of your two private cards must you use in your final five-card hand?
