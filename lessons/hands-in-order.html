LESSON 02 · HAND STRENGTH · 4 MIN

Hands, in order

Know the rankings, understand kickers and spot the best five.

From strongest to weakest

The categories are royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair and high card. A royal flush is the highest possible straight flush. Use the hand-ranking reference in the toolkit to compare examples.

Breaking a tie

When players share a category, compare the ranks that make that hand, then the remaining cards, called kickers, in descending order. With two pair, compare the higher pair first, then the lower pair, then the kicker. Suits never break ties in Texas Hold’em.

The ace can work two ways

An ace can be high in A-K-Q-J-10 or low in 5-4-3-2-A. A straight cannot wrap around from king to two. In a five-high straight, the five is the high card. A flush needs five cards of the same suit, but they do not need to be consecutive.

TAKE THIS WITH YOUCompare five-card hands, not just private cards. If the board is the best hand for everyone, the pot is split.
