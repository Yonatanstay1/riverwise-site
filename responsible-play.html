RESPONSIBLE PLAY · 18+

Keep the game in perspective.

Sopavo is for adults aged 18 or over. It teaches the game only: there is no real-money play, no deposits and no prizes on this site.

Habits that matter

  • Decide on a time limit before you start, and take regular breaks.
  • Judge your decisions by your reasoning, not by one result. Good decisions can still lose.
  • Never chase losses or raise stakes out of frustration.
  • Never borrow money to gamble, and never treat gambling as a way to make money.
  • If you ever gamble elsewhere, follow local laws and the minimum legal age where you are.

Warning signs

Spending more time or money than you meant to, hiding it from people close to you, feeling anxious or irritable when you are not playing, or gambling to escape problems are all signs to stop and talk to someone.

Free, confidential help

UK · 24 HOURSNational Gambling Helpline

Call 0808 8020 133 free, or chat online with GamCare.

UK · SELF-EXCLUSIONGAMSTOP

Block yourself from UK-licensed gambling sites and apps at gamstop.co.uk.

WORLDWIDEGambling Therapy

Free support in many languages at gamblingtherapy.org.

These are independent services. Sopavo does not run them and is not connected to them.

Under 18?

Sopavo is not for you. The site does not verify age, so we rely on you to leave. Parents can use device or browser filters to block gambling-related content.
