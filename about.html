ABOUT RIVERWISE

A clear, honest place
to learn poker.

Riverwise is a free, English-language site that teaches the fundamentals of Texas Hold’em to adults. It is run by Stay Innovation.

What is on the site

  • Six short lessons with a quick quiz each: the rules, hand rankings, position, pot odds, betting with a reason and healthy habits.
  • A hand-ranking guide from royal flush to high card, with an example of each.
  • A pot-odds calculator that shows the break-even equity for a simple call.

Everything works in your browser without an account. Lesson progress is saved only on your own device.

What is not on the site

  • No real-money play, deposits, withdrawals, prizes or paid entries.
  • No AI opponent. We have built a small practice lab of scripted, prewritten scenarios, not live AI. It is not on the public site while we confirm the age and market requirements that apply to it.
  • No affiliate links, bonus offers or advertising.

Independence

Riverwise is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by any poker or gambling operator. If we ever enter a commercial relationship, we will disclose it clearly beside the content it affects, before it starts.

No promised outcomes

The lessons simplify real situations. They are general education, not personal advice, and no strategy guarantees a win or a profit. Poker involves chance and risk.

Who runs Riverwise

Riverwise is run by Stay Innovation. You can reach us at yonatanzlit@stayinnovation.com. See the contact page for details.
