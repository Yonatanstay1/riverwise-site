01 / THE NUMBERS→

Know the price of a call.

Pot odds tell you the break-even equity for a call in a simple, no-further-betting example.

BREAK-EVEN EQUITY25%
1 in 4

Call 50 to contest a final pot of 200. You need to win 25% of the time to break even.

How the calculation works

Required equity = call ÷ (pot before the bet + opponent’s bet + your call). This ignores rake, future betting, implied odds and tournament considerations. The percentage is a break-even threshold, not your actual chance of winning.

A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Before you
take a seat.

Can I play for real money here?

No. Riverwise is an educational site. There are no deposits, withdrawals, prizes, real-money tables or links to gambling operators. Practice units have no cash value.

Is there an AI opponent or practice mode?

No. There is no AI opponent on Riverwise. We have built a small practice lab of scripted scenarios with prewritten feedback, but it is not on the public site while we confirm the applicable age and market requirements.

Who is this for?

Adults aged 18 or over who want to understand Texas Hold’em fundamentals. The site teaches the game and has no gambling of any kind. It does not verify age, so please do not use it if you are under 18.

Does better strategy guarantee a win?

No. Poker involves chance, incomplete information and risk. Educational examples simplify real decisions. A correct decision can still lose, and no strategy guarantees profit.

Is Riverwise affiliated with a poker operator?

No. Riverwise is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by any poker or gambling operator, and it has no affiliate links. If that ever changes, we will say so clearly next to the content concerned.

Where is my progress saved?

Lesson completions and practice results are saved in this browser’s local storage. There are no user accounts, email sign-ups, cookies or analytics. Clearing browser data removes your progress.

Poker education is for adults. Keep time limits, take breaks and never treat gambling as income. If gambling is affecting your wellbeing, seek independent help at Gambling Therapy →.
