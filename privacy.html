PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy, in plain English.

Sopavo has no accounts and uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. The only personal information we collect is an email address you choose to give us.

Who we are

Sopavo is run by Stay Innovation, which is responsible for this notice. Contact: yonatanzlit@stayinnovation.com.

Lesson progress on your device

When you complete a lesson, the site saves which lessons you finished in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads the page, and may keep it for security purposes under GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not receive or see this information.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the standard browser request needed to deliver them, under Google’s privacy policy.

Cheat sheet and lesson emails

If you sign up for the cheat sheet, we collect your email address, the page you signed up on, the time, and your confirmation that you are 18 or over. We use it only to send you the cheat sheet link and occasional emails about new lessons, at most twice a month. We never send gambling offers, operator promotions or bonus codes, and we never sell or share your address.

Sign-ups are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service, under its privacy policy. Our legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by replying “stop” to any email or writing to yonatanzlit@stayinnovation.com, and we will delete your address.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed. We do not add you to the mailing list unless you sign up for it.

External links

Links to support services open other websites, which have their own privacy practices.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing yonatanzlit@stayinnovation.com. If you are in the UK and unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, we will update this notice first. Last updated 27 November 2026.
