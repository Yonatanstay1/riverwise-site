Six things to remember

  1. Meet Texas Hold’em. Your hand is always the best five cards available to you. You do not have to use both private cards.
  2. Hands, in order. Compare five-card hands, not just private cards. If the board is the best hand for everyone, the pot is split.
  3. Position is information. Before making a decision, ask: who still has to act, and will I have position on later streets?
  4. The price of a call. Required equity = call amount ÷ final pot after your call. Compare this threshold with a reasoned estimate, not a hope.
  5. Bet with a reason. Before betting, finish this sentence: “I expect these worse hands to call,” or “I expect these better hands to fold.”
  6. Build healthy habits. Review the quality of your thinking, take breaks and keep limits. No lesson or strategy guarantees profit.
